CLUB FEATURES

Equinox Sports Club San Francisco’s luxurious, state-of-the-art facilities offer a comprehensive selection of fitness options. The club is fully equipped with weights, cardio equipment, plus Yoga and Pilates studios. At our Market Street location, you'll find an impressive Boxing Studio, full-sized indoor Basketball Court, and a junior Olympic-sized saline pool.

The Spa at Equinox

The Spa at our Equinox clubs is a personal recharging station, where you'll take your results further with bodywork, facials, and more. Book an appointment today. Open to non-members.

Personal Training

One-on-One training at Equinox is backed by our Health Advisory Board, and the EQX OS. This proprietary operating system, built to advance full health optimization, decodes and unlocks the peak of your potential, redefining High-Performance Living on a whole new scale.

Studio Pilates

Strengthen as you stretch with individual Studio Pilates and Virtual Pilates sessions, plus unlimited Mat Pilates classes. Our certified Pilates instructors will guide you through an intense, full-body workout emphasizing the core.

The Shop

Where fashion and fitness intersect. Pursue your results with activewear essentials and exclusive collaborations both in the club and on The Shop online.

Boxing Studio

Whether mastering technique in a high-intensity class or freestyling on your own, you'll find our well-appointed studio packs a serious punch.

Luxury Fitness in San Francisco

Equinox Sports Club San Francisco members enjoy access to amenities like an in-house Spa, Steam Rooms, a Pilates Studio, a Yoga Studio, and a Barre Studio. San Francisco’s best instructors take you through full-body workouts that use core strength exercises to sculpt and tone.
