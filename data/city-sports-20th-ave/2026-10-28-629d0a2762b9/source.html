ABOUT US

City Sports Club offers its visitors an invigorating diversity of options - from plenty of free weights and cardio machines, to personal training and a wide variety of group fitness classes, to a lap pool, indoor cycling, and basketball & racquetball courts.

Add to this our friendly staff and clean spacious facilities, and you'll see why City Sports Club is your lifestyle answer to healthy living.
