About Aquatics

Natatorium Hours of Operation 

  • Monday-Thursday: 10am- 8pm
  • Friday-Saturday: Noon- 4pm
  • Sunday: Closed

The Natatorium

Our natatorium offers two heated pools, a hot tub, a sauna, a deck shower, and fully equipped locker rooms. It is a beautifully lit space that serves as a central area for physical activity and socializing at the MWC. On the pool deck, a wide range of swim accessories such as noodles, kickboards, water weights, and pull buoys are available for use.

Lap Pool: 

Our Lap Pool is designed for serious lap swimmers and has six lanes with a depth of 7-10 feet. It is maintained at a comfortable 80 degrees and has an accessible entrance. The lap pool length is 25 meters for those who want to track their distance.

Activity Pool:

Our activity pool is located at 86 degrees, suitable for recreational swimmers, professional pool loungers, swim lessons, and Aqua Zumba classes. It is 0-4 feet deep and has an accessible entrance.

Hot Tub (Spa):

Our 24-person hot tub is maintained at an average of 101 degrees and features various adjustable jets. To ensure everyone's safety and health, we kindly request that you limit your time in the hot tub to 30 minutes or less.

Sauna: 

This sauna comfortably accommodates ten people. For safety and health reasons, we ask all participants to limit their time in the sauna to 15-20 minutes

Programming is accessible on the Member Portal.

For any questions, comments, or concerns, please contact our Aquatics Student Leads at aquatics@mail.sfsu.edu or our Aquatics & Safety Coordinator, Ben Forchini, at forchini@sfsu.edu.
