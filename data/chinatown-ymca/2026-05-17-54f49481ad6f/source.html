Our Facilities

Saltwater Pool

Our 5-lane, 25-yard, saltwater pool offers our members a place to take Aqua Fit, lap swim and take swim lessons.

Studio A

Studio A on the 2nd floor of our facility hosts our yoga, pilates, tai chi, and dance classes.

Studio B

Studio B on the 2nd floor of our facility hosts our cycling classes.

Basketball Court

The basketball court on the 2nd floor of our facility provides our members with a place to play basketball, badminton, volleyball or join in on a bootcamp or tai chi class.

Multi-Purpose Room

Our Multi-Purpose Room on the 2nd floor of our facility offers a place for our members to play ping pong, community groups to hold meetings, and our families to enjoy a Y-organized family dinner together.

Courtyard

Our 2nd floor outdoor courtyard provides a calm and quiet place for a rest after your workout or for a quick meal during the day.

Fitness Center

The fitness center on the 3rd floor of our facility includes stationary bikes, treadmills, and elliptical machines as well as free weights, medicine balls, strength machines, and an area to stretch or warm up.

Classroom Play Area

The 3rd floor classroom play area is available to members with young children and is well-stocked with toys and books for all ages.*

*When there is no program in the classroom.
