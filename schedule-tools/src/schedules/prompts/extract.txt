Return only structured data that matches the provided JSON schema.

For example, if the schedule covers May 12 – Jun 30 and the cancellation recurs on the 3rd Thursday, emit closures for 2026-05-21 and 2026-06-18, each with the partial-day time range.

- Preserve distinct sessions even if they share the same day or type.
